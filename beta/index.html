Why book a tour?

The museum is vast. A guide brings it to life.

The British Museum holds around 50,000 artefacts across nearly 100 galleries. Many of them relate directly to the Tenach and Jewish history, but they are easy to walk past. A knowledgeable guide finds them for you and brings them to life.

Ideal for
Students of Tenach
Visitors to London
Anyone interested in Jewish history
Anyone, of any background, with an interest in biblical archaeology and the Hebrew Bible
Your guide

Mrs Shoshana Tugendhaft

Shoshana has been guiding tours at the British Museum for over 12 years. She holds a degree in History (BA Hist hons) and has been teaching Tenach, Jewish history, and Jewish philosophy for over 25 years. She is a published author and has over 700 shiurim (lectures) published online.

Her tours bring that classroom enthusiasm into the galleries, joining the text to the artefacts that stand beside it.
