Why book a tour?

The museum is vast. A guide makes it speak.

The British Museum holds around 50,000 artefacts across nearly 100 galleries. Many of them touch the Tenach and Jewish history directly, but they are easy to walk past. A knowledgeable guide finds them for you and brings them to life.

Ideal for
1Students of Tenach
2Anyone interested in Jewish history
3Those curious about biblical archaeology
Your guide

Mrs Shoshana Tugendhaft

Shoshana holds a BA in History and studied ancient and medieval Jewish texts at the Michlala in Jerusalem. For more than 20 years she has taught Tenach, Jewish history and Jewish philosophy, in high schools and in adult education.

Her tours bring that classroom enthusiasm into the galleries, joining the text to the artefacts that stand beside it.
